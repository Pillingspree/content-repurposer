# Why Most Founders Should Repurpose Their Content

Creating content is expensive in one place: the idea. Once you have a good idea, the cost of showing it in a new format is small, yet most founders write one blog post and stop.

## One idea, many shapes

A strong blog post contains a hook, three or four key points and a takeaway. Each of those maps neatly onto another format. The hook becomes a LinkedIn opener. The key points become a thread or carousel slides. The takeaway becomes the call to action in an email.

## Keep the idea, change the format

The mistake is copying and pasting. LinkedIn readers skim short paragraphs. X rewards punchy, numbered points. Instagram needs one idea per slide. Email readers want a clear subject line and a single link to click.

## Start small

Pick your best-performing post this quarter and repurpose it into three formats this week. Measure which one brings the most replies, then do more of that.
